Youth Map Lab was designed as a program to help the National 4-H Geospatial Leadership Team fulfill it's grant-partnership commitments with NC A&T University and Google. 

However, under the direction of our leadership team- we'd like to make a commitment to build a toolkit for students and educators unlike any other. Our plan is to build a toolkit and collection of geospatial educational resources that includes Youth Map Lab workshops, activities, StoryMaps that can be easily used within any classroom, community center or club. 

We've opted to partner with Esri to help ensure that these experiences are available to all and are working directly with one of the largest
school systems in the United States; Wake County Public School System to ensure that our curriculum is usable, effective, and applies to real world 
scenarios.
