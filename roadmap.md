# 🌍 Youth Map Lab Roadmap (2025–2027)

Youth Map Lab began as a way to help the National 4-H Geospatial Leadership Team meet partnership goals with NC A&T and Google.  
It has since evolved into something much bigger:

- A teacher-friendly GIS toolkit that runs in any classroom in 45–90 minutes  
- A UDL-aligned, standards-aware resource library across multiple subjects  
- A student-built model supporting WCPSS’s ArcGIS expansion (2026–2027)  
- A national plug-and-play geospatial learning ecosystem for schools, libraries, and youth programs  

**Long-term mission:** Become the most accessible, student-centered geospatial education library in the United States.
